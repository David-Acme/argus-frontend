Argus: cosmic presence

Seven cosmic options for Argus' presence, drawn live on canvas. Each one has six states: idle, listening, thinking, speaking (pulsing with a simulated voice level, shown as a waveform), on watch, and asleep. Every option is shown at about 200 px on dark and on light, and at 28 and 36 px. The palette is warm sand and amber on deep indigo, aiming for calm, premium and futuristic. The states cycle on their own; pick one to hold it.
