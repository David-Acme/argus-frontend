Argus: mascot proposals

Argus will speak with a male voice: calm, warm and confident. These are six proposals for the character that goes with it: option 0 is today's figure evolved, and 1–5 are new concepts. Every one is drawn here as inline SVG with CSS, in the warm charcoal and sand palette, at every size the app uses (28 · 36 · 96–120 · 200 px), in light and dark, and in all 11 states. The main prototype keeps today's mascot until David chooses.

Comparativa a tamaños pequeños0 · Argus evolucionado1 · Panoptes2 · Búho3 · Can guardián4 · Orbe5 · Vigía
Recommendation: 2 · Búho (the owl), with 0 · Argus evolucionado as the continuity option. The owl is the most natural fit for a calm, grown-up male voice: a wise night watchman, neither childish nor intimidating. It stays legible at 28 px (two round eyes and two tufts), its "on watch" state is meaningful (the eyes glow amber and the tufts rise), and it rigs simply in Rive. It can borrow Panoptes' peacock eye as a small mark on its chest, which keeps the link to the name Argus. Option 0 changes least and keeps what David already likes, but even refined it reads younger than the voice.
