Argus: aura

Three proposals for Argus' presence as a free aura, with no circle and nothing to contain it. Each is a flow of light and particles with open edges that fade into the background. It drifts like a galaxy at rest, streams toward you when listening, braids or folds when thinking, flows in waves with the voice when speaking, opens into a calm gaze when on watch, and settles into a dim ribbon when asleep. Each is shown at large size on dark and on light, plus what it becomes at icon size.
