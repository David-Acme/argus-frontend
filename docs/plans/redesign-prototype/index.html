Argus: redesign prototype v3.1

v3 starts from the Argus of today: its warm charcoal and sand palette, the cream buttons, the cards with gold icon wells, the bold headings. The white mascot gives way to Estela, the presence David chose. It fixes what the findings list and adds a new-era layer. Each main screen shows today's capture next to the redesign. v3.1 adds: sheet and dialog actions in one pinned footer, compact heroes so content starts above the fold, 3–4 desktop columns with before and after at 1920 and 2560, per-column scrolling, and the loading rule (local data paints instantly, skeletons only for HTTP). Argus becomes a presence: an Aura that breathes with the home's state, Estela, a living stream of light, across the app, glass only on what floats, spring motion, and one signature gesture: hold the centre to talk. Every one of these is a single reusable primitive (see redesign-spec.md §4 and §5).

La identidad: una presencia, no un tablero

AuraA soft light field at the top of Inicio and Seguridad. Its colour and tempo come from the home's state: calm, on watch, attention, alert, offline. Static under reduced motion.
EstelaArgus' presence: a stream of stars and light that flows like a living brushstroke, with no body and no container. Eleven states driven by app state and the voice level. It draws an eye when Argus looks through a camera. One per screen, always beside words.
Mantén para hablarHold the centre tab from any screen. The screen's edge lights up, Estela streams toward you, you speak, and you let go. A tap opens the chat.
Vidrio solo en lo que flotaThe floating tab bar, sheets, the desktop sidebar and the command palette. Content stays solid and legible. Solid under reduced transparency.
Movimiento con físicaThree springs: snappy for presses, gentle for sheets, and a free flow for Estela. The camera tile grows into the live view.
Una frase grandeOne expressive line per screen: «Todo tranquilo.», «Buenas noches.», «Alguien en el patio.» Everything else stays quiet.
⌘K es ArgusOn desktop, one bar for commands, search and asking Argus. On the phone, the same thing behind the search button.

Estela: la presencia de Argus, once estados

The owner chose Estela (R-D28) from the aura proposals. It replaces the white mascot everywhere: a stream of stars and light with open edges that fade into the background, never a circle or a container. Each card shows it at 150 px and as the icon at 28, 36 and 48 px. It reacts to you: it leans toward your pointer or touch, it brightens when a message arrives (tap it to see), it drifts lightly with the scroll on Inicio, and it breathes slower and deeper while on watch. In the app it is one Presence component (Skia Atlas on phones, the same particle system on canvas on web and desktop) with inputs state and level. With «Reducir movimiento» each state becomes a still frame. It is decorative to screen readers: the words beside it carry the meaning.

Estela · catálogo de animaciones

Every case is a parameter of ONE Presence component: a state (idle, listen, think, speak, look, search, found, notfound, known, unknown, happy, error, alert, panic, watch, sleep, off), an event overlay (assemble, appear, disperse, cancel, ripple-in/out, notify, glint, modechange, reconnect, touch), an intensity and a target point (where the pupil looks). Escritorio (1280 × 820, Tauri / web)

A glass sidebar, ⌘K, keyboard shortcuts (1–4 for the sections, N for new, Esc to close), hover, and layouts that use the width. Dialogs close when you click outside them or press Esc. A form with unsaved text asks before it discards. Try it: open «Nuevo evento» and click the dark area, or press Ctrl/⌘+K.

Pantallas grandes: 1920 y 2560, con datos y con pocos datos

Today, at 2560, the content floats about 370 px from the rail and stops at around 1,640 px. v3.4 also fills the height: every column is a full-height panel, lists show as many items as fit, camera tiles stretch, the aside grows sections, and the sidebar ends with status and a quick prompt. «Pocos datos» shows the same screens with little data. The redesign adds columns as width grows: 3 up to 1699 px, 4 from 1700. Text columns keep readable widths (the feed at most 620 px, about 72 characters), the camera grid takes the extra width, and the content is capped at 2,200 px and centred, with the sidebar attached. Each column scrolls on its own with thin, consistent scrollbars, its section headers stick, and the band and the sidebar never scroll.
