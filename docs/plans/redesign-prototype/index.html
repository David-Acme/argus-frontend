Argus: redesign prototype v2

This version goes further than calm and clean. Argus becomes a presence: an Aura that breathes with the home's state, a mascot that is alive across the app, glass only on what floats, spring motion, and one signature gesture: hold the centre to talk. Every one of these is a single reusable primitive (see redesign-spec.md §4 and §5).

La identidad: una presencia, no un tablero

AuraA soft light field at the top of Inicio and Seguridad. Its colour and tempo come from the home's state: calm, on watch, attention, alert, offline. Static under reduced motion.
Argus, vivoOne mascot, eleven states, driven by app state and voice level. It wears the detective hat while it is on watch. At most one mascot per screen, always paired with words.
Mantén para hablarHold the centre tab from any screen. The screen's edge lights up, the mascot leans in, you speak, and you let go. A tap opens the chat.
Vidrio solo en lo que flotaThe floating tab bar, sheets, the desktop sidebar and the command palette. Content stays solid and legible. Solid under reduced transparency.
Movimiento con físicaThree springs: snappy for presses, gentle for sheets and the mascot's play. The camera tile grows into the live view.
Una frase grandeOne expressive line per screen: «Todo tranquilo.», «Buenas noches.», «Alguien en el patio.» Everything else stays quiet.
⌘K es ArgusOn desktop, one bar for commands, search and asking Argus. On the phone, the same thing behind the search button.

Argus, la mascota: once estados

This is the existing figure (body, eyes, deerstalker hat, lens), redrawn here as inline SVG. In the app it is one Presence component backed by a Rive state machine with inputs state and level (voice amplitude). Under reduced motion each state is a still pose. The mascot is decorative to screen readers, because the words beside it always carry the meaning.

Tokens

One neutral ramp, one accent (arena), three status colours, and five aura tones that are tints, never text. Six type steps: hero 36 is the expressive moment, used once per screen. Spacing is 4·8·12·16·24·32·48, radii 8·14·22·28·full, and the springs are snappy, gentle and playful.

Teléfono (claro)

360 × 760. A floating glass bar with Inicio · Seguridad · Argus · Agenda · Tú. Primary actions sit in the lower half, within thumb reach. Try it: press and hold the mascot in the centre of any bar.

Detalles, hojas, diálogos y estados

A sheet closes when you tap outside it: try it on «Hoja inferior», and «Abrir de nuevo» brings it back. There are three error surfaces, chosen by context (spec §5.8), and the low-end Android tier. The other frames show a native alert, a skeleton in the final layout, an empty state with the mascot and an undo toast, stale data shown honestly, and text at 130%.

Modo oscuro

The aura becomes a low glow and the mascot stays luminous. «Buenas noches» shows night mode: the hat is on, because Argus is on watch.

Escritorio (1280 × 820, Tauri / web)

A glass sidebar, ⌘K, keyboard shortcuts (1–4 for the sections, N for new, Esc to close), hover, and layouts that use the width. Dialogs close when you click outside them or press Esc. A form with unsaved text asks before it discards. Try it: open «Nuevo evento» and click the dark area, or press Ctrl/⌘+K.
